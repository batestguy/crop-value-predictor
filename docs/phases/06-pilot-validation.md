# Stage 6 — Literature-Informed Calculator Readiness

**Status:** Approved — literature-informed readiness (user decision 2026-09-09)
**Dependency:** Stage 5 approved 2026-09-07; Stage 6 user approval recorded 2026-09-09

## Objective

Use academic and authoritative evidence to establish calculator design
requirements, adoption risks, literacy/accessibility considerations, and a
future evaluation method. This stage does not substitute academic evidence for
direct users: participant validation remains absent.

The Stage 1 source gate still blocks Stages 3 and 4. Automated prices,
source-driven rankings, and forecasting remain separately gated and unchanged.

## Work and evidence completed

- Review protocol, scope, exact search strings, databases, search date,
  eligibility/exclusion rules, screening counts, quality-appraisal method, and
  limitations are recorded in
  [academic-evidence-review.md](../academic-evidence-review.md).
- The extraction matrix has 20 sources with stable DOI or URL references,
  including five sources in each of the four evidence areas:
  [academic-evidence-matrix.csv](../academic-evidence-matrix.csv).
- Two review passes checked eligibility, citation identity, extraction wording,
  evidence tiers, and the distinction between literature-informed requirements
  and product-specific validation. The second pass is an internal audit, not
  external peer review.
- Every major concern—units, validation, ranking interpretation, price-range
  meaning, offline persistence, low bandwidth, accessibility, language, and
  claim boundaries—is mapped to a requirement, warning, automated test, or
  unresolved risk.
- The prior participant protocol and session kit remain available as deferred
  human-validation materials. They are superseded for the current milestone and
  are not active gate evidence.

## Acceptance gate

The Stage 6 gate review requirements are satisfied: the review protocol, search log,
screening record, extraction matrix, references, quality/context judgments,
two review passes, and concern-to-requirement map are complete; stable source
links are present; and product claims are labelled direct, transferable,
contextual, or insufficient.

The gate explicitly requires that no literature finding be represented as proof
of Nigerian farmer comprehension, usefulness, completion rate, offline recovery,
or real-device performance. The 80% completion and 70% helpfulness thresholds
are removed because there is no participant denominator in this milestone.

**Decision:** Approved — literature-informed readiness, recorded after the user's
explicit approval on 2026-09-09. The status must never be farmer-validated.

## Deferred human validation

Direct participant work remains a future milestone. If authorized later, it must
use the retained materials, a new approved protocol, a defined sampling and
consent plan, participant-level privacy controls, and separate measures for task
completion, unit/ranking comprehension, interpretation of price ranges,
helpfulness, and device/offline behavior. No future participant result is
created or implied by this gate.
